INature was founded in Bristol in December 2023 by Seyda and Ferhat — a couple who wanted to bring a familiar companion with them on their new chapter in the UK.

Back in Türkiye, they had always cherished using INCIA products. The natural ingredients, the environmentally friendly practices, and the everyday sense of wellness they provided had become indispensable to their daily lives — and they couldn't imagine leaving them behind.

Your health is our responsibility

When they settled in the UK, Seyda and Ferhat noticed how hard it was for friends and new families around them to find genuinely natural, safe products — especially for babies and sensitive skin. They realised that sharing INCIA with their new community could bring the same joy and health benefits to others. And so the idea of introducing INCIA to the UK was born.

Their journey began with a deep-seated passion for natural, sustainable living — the same passion that sits at the heart of INCIA. Every product we offer is a testament to that commitment: crafted with care, and designed to nurture and protect both our bodies and the environment. We believe everyone deserves access to pure, high-quality personal care that's free from harmful chemicals and synthetic ingredients.

What INature means

INature is a declaration of belonging. The "I" in our name is a personal space for you to define your relationship with the world:

  • I am Nature — recognising that our bodies and our health are inseparable from the earth.
  • I love Nature — celebrating the botanical ingredients that nourish and heal us.
  • I protect Nature — committing to sustainability and a cleaner future.

Our values

Our mission is simple: to bring the essence of INCIA to the UK, offering products that promote a healthier, more conscious lifestyle. As the official UK distributor of INCIA Naturals, every product we stock is 100% natural in origin, dermatologically tested, and certified — ETKO Cosmos Natural, Ecocert, Vegan Society, Leaping Bunny cruelty-free, Halal and GMP. The same formulas already trusted across Europe, including on the shelves of retailers like ETOS in the Netherlands.

Thank you for joining us on this journey. Explore our collection, experience the difference of natural, eco-friendly personal care, and become part of the INature family. Together, we can make everyday life a little more natural, a little more sustainable — and a lot more joyful.
