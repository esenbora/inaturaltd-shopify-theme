This Privacy Policy explains how Inature Limited ("INature", "we", "us") collects, uses and protects your personal data when you visit or make a purchase from this website. We are the data controller, registered in England and Wales (company number 15360311). We are committed to handling your data lawfully, transparently and in line with UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

What we collect

  • Information you give us — name, email address, delivery and billing address, phone number and order details when you place an order or create an account.
  • Payment information — processed securely by our payment providers. We do not store your full card details.
  • Information collected automatically — device, browser, IP address and how you use our site, via cookies and similar technologies.

How we use your data

  • To process and deliver your orders, and provide customer support.
  • To manage your account and send service messages (order confirmations, dispatch updates).
  • To send marketing emails where you have opted in — you can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To improve our website, products and service, and to keep the site secure.
  • To meet our legal and accounting obligations.

Legal basis

We process your data on the basis of: performance of our contract with you (to fulfil your order); your consent (for marketing and non-essential cookies); our legitimate interests (to run and improve our business securely); and legal obligation (e.g. tax records).

Who we share it with

We only share your data with trusted partners who help us run our store, including: our e-commerce platform (Shopify), payment providers, and our delivery partner (Evri). These providers process data on our behalf under appropriate safeguards. We never sell your personal data.

Cookies

We use cookies to make the site work, remember your basket, understand site usage and (with your consent) support marketing. You can manage non-essential cookies via the cookie banner or your browser settings.

Data retention

We keep your personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes above, including to satisfy legal, accounting or reporting requirements.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal data, to object to processing, and to data portability. You can also withdraw consent for marketing at any time. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@inatureltd.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email us at info@inatureltd.com.
