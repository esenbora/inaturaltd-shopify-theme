Brand & origins

Who is INCIA, and what does INature do?

INCIA is a Turkish natural personal care brand known for its clean, skin-friendly formulations. INature is the official UK distributor of INCIA, bringing certified, 100% natural products to families across the UK.

What does the name "INature" mean?

INature is a declaration of belonging. The "I" is a personal space for you to define your own relationship with the world — I am Nature, I love Nature, I protect Nature.

When and where was INature founded?

INature was founded in December 2023 in Bristol, UK, by Seyda and Ferhat — after they saw how hard it was for friends and new families in the UK to find safe, natural baby and personal care. We operate within the HemNature network, the same group behind INCIA's European presence (including retailers such as ETOS in the Netherlands).

Products & quality

Are your products really 100% natural?

Yes. Every INCIA formula is 100% natural in origin — plant-derived actives, mineral filters and botanical oils — free from SLS, parabens, mineral oils and synthetic fragrances. And it's certified, not just claimed: ETKO Cosmos Natural, Ecocert, Vegan Society, Leaping Bunny cruelty-free, Halal and GMP.

Are they safe for babies, pregnancy and sensitive skin?

Our range is dermatologically tested and developed with sensitive skin in mind, including formulas suitable for babies, children and during pregnancy. If you have a specific condition or concern, we always recommend checking the ingredient list and patch-testing first.

Are your products cruelty-free and vegan?

Always. Every product we stock is 100% cruelty-free, carrying the Leaping Bunny standard, and our range is Vegan Society approved. Read our guide to spotting genuinely cruelty-free skincare →
