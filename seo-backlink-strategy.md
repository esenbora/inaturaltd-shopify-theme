# INature UK — Off-Page / Backlink Strategy

## Where we are

Young domain (brand founded Dec 2023, .co.uk recently primary). Technically clean and indexed, but ranks mostly for brand terms (incia / inature). Non-brand terms (natural deodorant, mineral sunscreen, baby skincare) sit on page 3+ with tiny impression volume. The gap is not on-page (that is done and data-aligned) — it is **authority / referring domains**. This plan builds that.

**Constraints:** small team, do-it-yourself, low budget. So: relevance over volume, earn over buy, leverage assets we actually have.

**Assets to leverage (this is our edge):** official UK distributor of INCIA (exclusive angle), real certifications (ETKO Cosmos Natural, Ecocert, The Vegan Society, Leaping Bunny cruelty-free, BRCGS, GMP, Zero Waste), founder story (Seyda and Ferhat, Bristol, son's eczema), HemNature European network + ETOS (NL), eBay 100% positive feedback, and genuinely useful pillar content.

**Rule of thumb:** a handful of links from UK natural-beauty / parenting / eco sites beats 100 directory blasts. Never buy link packages or use PBNs (Google penalty + wasted money).

---

## Tier 1 — Foundation (Week 1, free, do first)

Trust + entity signals. Fast, safe, foundational.

1. **Google Business Profile (Bristol)** — even online-only. Verify it. Single strongest free trust + brand-SERP signal. Add Bing Places + Apple Business Connect too.
2. **Consistent citations (same name/address everywhere):** Yell, FreeIndex, Cylex, Hotfrog, Scoot, Thomson Local. Use the Stimilon LLC / INature details consistently.
3. **The exclusivity link:** get listed as the **official UK stockist/distributor** on INCIA's own site and the HemNature network. A relevant, high-trust link from the brand you distribute. Ask Ferhat to request it from INCIA/HemNature.
4. **Certification directory listings (we are entitled to these):** The Vegan Society trademark directory, Leaping Bunny / Cruelty Free International brand listing, Ecocert / COSMOS certified-brand pages. High-relevance, high-trust, and we already hold the certs.
5. **Social profiles fully built + linked back:** Instagram (@inatureuk), TikTok, Facebook, LinkedIn company page, YouTube, and especially **Pinterest** (natural-skincare pins drive real referral traffic + links). Trustpilot + eBay store + Google Merchant already exist — make sure each links to inatureltd.co.uk.

---

## Tier 2 — Niche relevance (Weeks 2-6)

Links from UK natural-beauty / eco / parenting sites — these move non-brand rankings.

1. **Product-roundup outreach (highest ROI):** email authors of "best natural deodorant UK", "best mineral sunscreen for kids", "best natural nappy cream UK" listicles and ask to be included. These are the exact non-brand terms we want to rank for, and the link comes from a page already ranking for them.
2. **UK parenting/baby blogs — gifting:** send the baby range (nappy rash gel, baby oil, shampoo) for honest reviews. Target Tots100-listed mum bloggers and UK parenting blogs. Baby/eczema angle is our strongest story.
3. **Vegan / cruelty-free / ethical directories + blogs:** Cruelty Free Kitty, Ethical Consumer, vegan-beauty UK bloggers. Pitch the Vegan Society + Leaping Bunny credentials.
4. **Eco / zero-waste UK blogs:** pitch the Zero Waste certification + reef-safe, non-nano mineral SPF angle.
5. **Local Bristol:** Bristol independent-retailer / founder features, Bristol lifestyle blogs, "Bristol startups" roundups. Local relevance + a real story.

---

## Tier 3 — Digital PR / content-led (ongoing, biggest ceiling)

Earn editorial links with stories and expertise.

1. **Founder-story pitch:** "Bristol couple bring Turkish natural skincare to the UK after their child's eczema." Human, local, linkable. Pitch to Bristol press + UK parenting/lifestyle outlets.
2. **Expert commentary (free links from journalists):** register Seyda/Ferhat as sources on ResponseSource, Featured (formerly HARO), Qwoted, and watch #JournoRequest / #PRequest on X. Respond to skincare / natural-beauty / eczema / sun-safety requests. Each pickup = an authority link.
3. **Linkable content we already have:** the pillar guides (certifications explained, mineral vs chemical, aluminium-free deodorant, baby skincare). Promote them. Consider one simple **free tool as linkbait** — e.g. an "ingredient / aluminium checker" or "is my sunscreen reef-safe?" widget. Tools attract links far better than articles.
4. **Network cross-links:** HemNature, ETOS, and INCIA — legitimate, relevant links within the distribution network.

---

## Tier 4 — Relationships (ongoing)

1. **Micro-influencer gifting:** UK natural-beauty + mum micro-influencers (5k-50k). Product for content + a link/mention. Cheaper and more authentic than big influencers.
2. **Guest posts:** offer the pillar topics as guest articles to UK natural-beauty / parenting blogs, with one natural contextual link.
3. **Community (referral + brand, not spam):** genuinely helpful participation in r/SkincareAddictionUK, UK parenting forums. No link-dropping — value first.

---

## Avoid (penalty / waste)

- Paid link packages, Fiverr backlinks, PBNs, link exchanges at scale.
- Irrelevant directory blasts.
- Exact-match anchor over-optimisation. Keep anchors natural: brand name, bare URL, and varied phrases.

---

## Priority + cadence

| When | Focus | Cost |
|------|-------|------|
| Week 1 | Tier 1: GBP + citations + INCIA/HemNature stockist link + cert directories + social/Pinterest | Free |
| Weeks 2-6 | Tier 2: product-roundup outreach + parenting-blog gifting + vegan/eco listings | Product samples |
| Ongoing | Tier 3 PR (founder story + journalist requests) + Tier 4 gifting | Time |

Target early: ~5-10 relevant referring domains in the first 6 weeks. Quality over count.
