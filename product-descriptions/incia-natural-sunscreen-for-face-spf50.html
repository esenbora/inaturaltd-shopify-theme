The INCIA Natural Sunscreen for Face SPF 50+ pairs daily mineral sun protection with skin-loving hydration in one lightweight, 100% natural formula. It uses non-nano, Ecocert-certified titanium dioxide to reflect UVA and UVB rays at the surface of the skin, rather than the chemical filters that are absorbed into it, so it is a clean choice for everyday wear.

Enriched with hyaluronic acid to draw in and hold onto moisture, plus nourishing tamanu oil, it helps keep the face hydrated and comfortable while it protects. The texture is light and non-greasy, it does not clog pores, and it works beautifully as a daily moisturiser with SPF. Hypoallergenic, dermatologically tested and free from synthetic fragrance, parabens and colourants, it suits every skin type, including sensitive, oily and blemish-prone skin.

How to use

Smooth a thin layer over the whole face, using roughly a pea-sized amount, and blend well. Reapply as needed, especially after swimming, towelling off or heavy perspiration, and ideally every two hours of sun exposure. Take care to avoid contact with fabrics.

Frequently asked questions

Does the face sunscreen leave a white cast?
Because it is a mineral sunscreen with titanium dioxide as the filter, you may notice a light white sheen after applying. This is normal with mineral sunscreens. This face formula has a lighter texture than the body version, and applying a thin layer and blending well helps keep any cast to a minimum.

Is it suitable for oily or blemish-prone skin?
Yes. The face formula has a light texture and does not clog the pores, helped by the hyaluronic acid. It is also free from synthetic fragrance and other synthetic additives.

Is the sunscreen water-resistant?
Yes, the face sunscreen is waterproof. We recommend reapplying every two hours, and after swimming, towelling off or heavy perspiration.

Can I wear it under make-up?
Yes. Its lightweight, non-greasy finish makes it easy to wear as the last step of your skincare and under make-up. For any ongoing skin concern, check with your GP or pharmacist.
