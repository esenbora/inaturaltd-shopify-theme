INCIA Natural Dishwashing Soap is a plant-based, biodegradable washing-up liquid that cuts through grease while staying kind to your hands. Made with more than 30% plant-derived soap from olive, coconut and castor oils, it cleans dishes thoroughly without the harsh synthetic detergents found in many conventional products. It is fragrance-free, free from artificial colours and a good fit for a lower-waste, more natural home.

Everyday washing-up liquids can leave hands feeling dry and tight. This formula uses a Super-Fat approach that helps condition the skin as you wash, so your hands stay more comfortable through a sink full of dishes. It is gentle enough to use without gloves, safe for cleaning baby bottles, teats and feeding accessories, and fully biodegradable, with 100% recyclable packaging and no animal-derived ingredients.

How to use

Apply a little washing-up liquid to a damp sponge or cloth, or add a squeeze to a bowl of warm water, then wash your dishes as usual. Rinse with clean water. Suitable for everyday washing-up as well as baby bottles, teats and other feeding accessories. For hand washing only, not for use in a dishwasher.

Full ingredients: Aqua, Potassium Olivate, Potassium Cocoate, Potassium Castorate, Acetic Acid, Sodium Bicarbonate, Glycerin, Parfum, Citrus Aurantium Dulcis (Orange) Peel Oil, Citrus Paradisi (Grapefruit) Peel Oil, Limonene.

  • Potassium Olivate, Cocoate and Castorate: plant-based soaps made from olive, coconut and castor oils that lift away grease.

  • Citrus peel oils (orange and grapefruit): naturally derived citrus oils that give the formula its fresh, clean character.
  • Sodium Bicarbonate: a familiar natural ingredient that supports effective cleaning.

Frequently asked questions

Is it safe for washing baby bottles and teats?
Yes. The plant-based formula is free from harsh synthetic detergents and artificial fragrance, so it suits baby bottles, teats and feeding accessories. Rinse well with clean water after washing.

Will it dry out my hands?
It is designed to be kinder to skin than conventional washing-up liquids. The Super-Fat formula helps condition your hands as you wash, and it is gentle enough to use without gloves.

Does it leave any smell or residue on dishes?
No. It is fragrance-free and rinses away cleanly, leaving no scent or taste behind on your dishes.

Can I use it in a dishwasher?
No. This is a washing-up liquid for hand washing only. It is not formulated for dishwasher use.
