Frequently asked questions

Does such a mild formula still cope with stronger odour?
Yes. Alum is a natural mineral salt used for generations to keep body odour at bay. Fresh sweat does not smell; the odour comes from bacteria on the skin, and alum helps tackle that effectively, even on more active days. If you are very active, you may like to reapply.

How long does it last through the day?
For the best result we suggest applying it the evening before, on clean, dry underarms, so it can work overnight and keep you feeling fresh the following day.

Can I use it during pregnancy or breastfeeding?
The formula contains no synthetic aluminium, alcohol or harsh ingredients. If you are pregnant or breastfeeding and have any doubts, we always recommend checking with your GP or midwife first.

Does it block my pores?
No. This is a deodorant, not an antiperspirant. You can still sweat, which is your body's natural way of cooling and cleansing itself. It simply neutralises the bacteria that cause unpleasant odour.
