INCIA Lip Balm Bergamot & Lemon is a 100% natural lip balm that keeps your lips soft, hydrated and protected all day, lifted by a fresh citrus scent from natural bergamot and lemon oils. Made entirely from plant-based ingredients, it delivers proper lip care in every kind of weather, with no synthetic fragrances, dyes or preservatives.

Beeswax forms a fine protective layer that helps stop lips drying out and shields them from harsh conditions, from winter wind to dry indoor heating and strong summer sun. Shea butter, coconut oil, castor oil and apricot kernel oil bring a high level of nourishment, keeping lips supple, glossy and comfortable through the day. It is a naturally derived, cruelty-free balm that suits everyday use, whatever the season.

How to use

Begin with clean, dry lips so the balm settles evenly. Twist the base of the stick to bring the balm up, then glide a thin layer over your lips, starting in the centre and working out to the corners. If you like, massage it in gently with small circular movements to help it absorb. Reapply through the day whenever lips feel dry or need extra care, particularly in cold or windy weather.

Frequently asked questions

Is it suitable for children as well as adults?
Yes. With 100% natural ingredients and no artificial additives, it suits both adults and children. With very young children we suggest supervising use. For a persistent concern, check with your GP or pharmacist.

Is it free from synthetic fragrances and colours?
Yes. There are no artificial fragrances or colours; the scent comes entirely from the natural bergamot and lemon oils in the formula.

Can it be used on very dry or chapped lips?
Yes, it is well suited to dry and chapped lips. The beeswax forms a protective layer that helps prevent moisture loss, while shea butter, coconut oil and apricot kernel oil soften and comfort. If lips stay very sore or cracked, speak to your GP or pharmacist.

Does the citrus scent come from essential oils?
Yes. The refreshing bergamot and lemon scent comes from natural essential oils, with no synthetic fragrance added.
