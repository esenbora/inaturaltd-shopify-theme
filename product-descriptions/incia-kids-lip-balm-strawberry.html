INCIA Kids Lip Balm Strawberry is a 100% natural lip balm made especially for children, with a sweet, naturally derived strawberry scent that little ones love. The gentle formula brings together shea butter, coconut oil, olive oil, calendula oil and Lady's Mantle (alchemilla) root extract, all chosen to soothe and protect delicate young lips without any synthetic fragrances, paraffin or artificial colours.

Children's lips have no oil-producing glands of their own, so they lose moisture fast and chap quickly in cold, dry or sunny weather. This soft balm lays down a comforting, protective layer that helps keep lips soft and looked after through busy days at nursery, school and play. The calendula and Lady's Mantle are traditional, calming botanicals, and because every ingredient is naturally derived, a small accidental lick is nothing to worry about.

How to use

Make sure your child's lips are clean and dry, then twist the base of the stick to bring the balm up. Help them apply a thin, even layer, starting in the middle of the lips and working outwards with light pressure so it spreads evenly. Reapply through the day whenever lips feel dry, especially in dry, cold or sunny weather. With very young children, apply it for them or supervise closely.

Frequently asked questions

From what age can my child use it?
It is made especially for children, with 100% natural ingredients and no artificial additives. With very young children we suggest you apply it for them or supervise use. For a persistent lip concern, check with your GP or pharmacist.

Is it free from synthetic fragrances and colours?
Yes. There are no artificial fragrances or colours; the scent and flavour come entirely from natural ingredients.

Can it be used on very dry or chapped lips?
Yes, it is well suited to dry and chapped lips. Natural ingredients such as beeswax and shea butter soften and comfort while forming a protective layer that helps prevent moisture loss. If lips stay very sore, speak to your GP or pharmacist.

Is it safe if my child licks it off?
Every ingredient is naturally derived, so a small amount licked or swallowed is nothing to worry about. It is still meant for the lips, so supervise younger children, and if in doubt check with your GP.
