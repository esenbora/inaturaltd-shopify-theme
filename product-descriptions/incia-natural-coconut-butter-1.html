How to use

Warm a small amount between your fingers until it melts, then apply. On skin, smooth it over rough, dry patches, elbows, knees, heels or anywhere that feels tight, and use it as a lip balm whenever lips feel dry. On hair, work it through the ends to help prevent split ends, or apply more generously as a pre-wash hair mask, leave it for 20 to 30 minutes and then shampoo out. A little dab is enough for hydration, protection and nourishment, so start small.

Frequently asked questions

Can I really use it on my face and lips?
Yes. It works well as a lip balm and on dry patches, and it forms a protective layer without clogging the pores. If your face is prone to congestion, try it on the body and lips first, or patch test.

How do I use it on my hair?
Either as a light finish on dry ends, or more generously as a mask before washing. The natural oils create a protective layer that helps hair hold moisture, so ends look smoother and split ends are less obvious.

Why does it change texture?
It is a natural butter, so it firms up when cold and softens in a warm room. Warm a little between your fingers and it melts straight onto the skin.

Does it smell artificial?
No. The coconut scent comes from the ingredients themselves; there are no artificial fragrances or colours in the jar.
