INCIA Lip Balm Coconut is a 100% natural moisturising lip balm that wraps your lips in a warm, tropical coconut scent and rich, lasting nourishment. Built around shea butter, coconut oil, olive oil, castor oil and apricot kernel oil, it protects lips from harsh weather while keeping them soft and glossy, with no paraffin, no parabens and no synthetic colours.

Beeswax lays down a fine protective layer that helps stop lips drying out, shielding them from cold winds, dry indoor heating and strong sun. The generous blend of natural oils and butters delivers genuine, long-lasting moisture rather than a film that fades in minutes, which makes this a great pick for tough UK winters. The uplifting coconut scent comes from natural parfum and essential oils, so every application feels like a small treat.

How to use

Start with clean, dry lips so the balm settles evenly. Twist the base of the stick to bring the balm up, then glide a thin layer over your lips, working from the centre out to the corners. If you like, massage it in gently with small circular movements to help it absorb. Reapply through the day whenever lips feel dry or need extra care, especially in cold or windy weather.

Frequently asked questions

Is it suitable for children as well as adults?
Yes. With 100% natural ingredients and no artificial additives, it suits both adults and children. With very young children we suggest supervising use. For a persistent concern, check with your GP or pharmacist.

Is it free from synthetic dyes and fragrances?
It contains no synthetic colours. The scent comes from natural parfum and essential oils. It does include limonene, linalool, benzyl salicylate and citral, which are natural fragrance components that occur in plant oils. If you know you are sensitive to these, patch test first or check with your GP or pharmacist.

Can it be used on very dry or chapped lips?
Yes, it is well suited to dry and chapped lips. The beeswax forms a protective layer that helps prevent moisture loss, while shea butter, coconut oil and olive oil soften and comfort. If lips stay very cracked or sore, speak to your GP or pharmacist.

Why choose a natural balm over a paraffin-based one?
Many high-street balms rely on paraffin and synthetic additives. This natural blend of oils and butters is designed to give lasting comfort rather than just a temporary film, so lips feel genuinely cared for.
