INCIA Natural Baby Oil is a 100% natural, hypoallergenic oil made for newborns and babies with delicate, sensitive skin. It brings together cold-pressed plant oils, including evening primrose, calendula and apricot kernel, into one gentle blend that nourishes, moisturises and looks after soft baby skin without harsh chemicals, parabens or synthetic antioxidants.

A baby's skin is thinner and more sensitive than adult skin, so it is worth keeping what you put on it as simple and natural as possible. This oil is dermatologically tested and rich in caring plant oils and vitamin E, making it lovely for daily massage, for smoothing into the skin after a bath and for taming fluffy hair. It is gentle enough for babies and children, and grown-ups can happily use it too.

  • 100% natural and hypoallergenic, dermatologically tested for sensitive skin
  • Moisturises and nourishes newborn and baby skin

How to use

Warm a little oil between your palms first, then massage it gently into your baby's skin. For an extra soft feel, add a few drops to the bath water. You can also smooth a small amount through hair to keep it soft and tame fluffiness. Suitable for babies, children and adults alike. As with any new product on very sensitive skin, patch test first.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use it on my baby's face?
Yes. The oil is hypoallergenic, dermatologically tested and developed for delicate baby skin, including the face. Use a small amount and, for a new product, patch test first. For a persistent skin concern, check with your GP or pharmacist.

Can adults use it too, on skin or hair?
Absolutely. The oil is suitable for babies, children and adults. Smooth it into dry skin or work a little through the ends of your hair.

Will it leave marks on clothing or bedding?
As with any oil, use a small amount and let it absorb fully before dressing your baby or putting them down, so there is less chance of transfer onto fabric.

Is it free from synthetic additives?
Yes. The formula is made with 100% natural ingredients, with no synthetic antioxidants, parabens or synthetic fragrance.
