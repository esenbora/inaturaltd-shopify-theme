The INCIA Nourishing and Hydrating Gel is made for dry, sensitive skin, including delicate baby skin, that needs a little extra care. It is hypoallergenic, dermatologically tested and packed with natural oils, with a rich, oily texture that intensely feeds moisture-hungry skin. Made from 100% natural ingredients, it is a comforting choice for skin that feels dry, tight or easily irritated.

Dry, eczema-prone skin often feels itchy, rough and flaky, and that can be especially uncomfortable for babies. Keeping skin well hydrated helps it feel calmer and more comfortable. This gel is rich in beeswax, shea butter and natural plant oils known for their moisturising and protective qualities, hydrating the skin without clogging pores. For any persistent or severe skin concern, always check with your GP or pharmacist.

How to use

Suitable for both babies and adults, the gel absorbs quickly. Apply it two to three times a day to dry or irritated areas; even a thin layer helps hydrate moisture-poor skin. Make sure skin is clean first, and remember the texture is quite oily. For ongoing or severe skin concerns, speak to your GP or pharmacist.

Ingredients

Full ingredients: Olea Europaea (Olive) Fruit Oil, Cera Alba, Soybean Glycerides, Butyrospermum Parkii (Shea) Butter, Hypericum Perforatum Extract, Hippophae Rhamnoides Fruit Oil, Oenothera Biennis (Evening Primrose) Oil, Borago Officinalis Seed Oil, Asiaticoside, Madecassic Acid, Asiatic Acid, Tocopherol.   • Beeswax: hydrating and comforting, it forms a light protective layer on the skin and brings beneficial acids and vitamin A.
  • Shea butter: rich in vitamins A and E, deeply nourishing and helps skin feel renewed and supple.
  • Centella asiatica actives (asiatic acid): long used in traditional plant care, valued for helping skin feel supported and comforted.

Frequently asked questions

Is it suitable for daily use on dry or sensitive skin?
Yes. The gel is hypoallergenic, dermatologically tested and suitable for babies and adults with dry or sensitive skin. We recommend continued use to keep skin nourished and hydrated. Apply two to three times a day to dry or irritated areas.

Can I use it on my face as well as my body?
Yes. It can be used on the face too. Just make sure your face is clean, then apply a thin layer to dry or irritated areas. Do keep in mind that the texture is quite oily.

Can it help dry, red or itchy skin?
It is made to comfort dry skin and soothe the feel of itchiness and the look of redness. Hydrating dry, irritated skin well can help it feel calmer, thanks to comforting beeswax, nourishing shea butter and Centella asiatica actives. For persistent or severe skin concerns, always check with your GP, pharmacist or dermatologist.

Is it suitable to use during pregnancy?
The gel is made from 100% natural ingredients with no synthetic additives. Even so, during pregnancy we always recommend checking with your GP or midwife before trying a new product, natural or otherwise, as they can advise what is right for you.
