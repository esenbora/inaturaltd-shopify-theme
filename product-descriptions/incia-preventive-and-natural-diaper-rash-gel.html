The INCIA Preventive and Natural Nappy Rash Gel is made for the softest little bottoms. It is packed with natural, nourishing ingredients that work together to protect against nappy rash while supporting the skin's own ability to recover, all with a lovely natural scent that comes purely from its plant oils.

It is a gentle, do-it-all baby balm. As well as helping with the redness and irritation of nappy rash, it doubles as a handy first-aid gel for the little scrapes and bumps that come with learning to crawl and walk, thanks to St John's Wort oil. Hypoallergenic and dermatologically tested, it is made with 100% natural ingredients and suits even the most delicate baby skin.

How to use

At each nappy change, apply a small amount of the gel to clean, dry skin. A thin layer already brings plenty of comfort, and it absorbs well. Regular use helps prevent nappy rash, eases redness and nourishes the skin. For severe or persistent rash or eczema, always speak to your GP, health visitor or a dermatologist.

Frequently asked questions

Does the gel contain fragrance or alcohol?
No. The gel contains no alcohol and no synthetic fragrance or preservatives. Every ingredient is of natural origin, and the pleasant scent comes purely from the plant oils in the formula, such as calendula, St John's Wort and jojoba. It is formulated to be hypoallergenic, so it suits even the most delicate baby skin.

Is it safe for babies with eczema or sensitive skin?
Yes. The gel is hypoallergenic and developed especially for delicate baby skin. The natural oils and shea butter help support the skin's ability to recover and soothe it where needed, and it is suitable for the very youngest. For severe or persistent eczema, we always recommend seeing your GP or a dermatologist.

Does it help with existing nappy rash, or is it only preventive?
Both. The gel helps protect and care for the skin whether you are heading off nappy rash or easing existing irritation. Applied regularly, it also helps prevent it coming back.

How often can I use it?
You can apply a thin layer at each nappy change. For an ongoing concern, check with your GP or health visitor.
