How to use

Warm a small amount between your fingers, then massage into clean skin in slow circles until it has sunk in. It works best on damp skin straight after a bath or shower, when it helps trap that moisture. Use it morning, evening or both, concentrating on tummy, hips, thighs, bust, upper arms, knees and elbows, or anywhere that feels dry. A little goes a long way. Patch test first if your skin is very sensitive.

Frequently asked questions

Will it get rid of my stretch marks?
No skincare product can remove stretch marks, and we will not pretend otherwise. What this butter does is keep skin richly moisturised so it feels supple and comfortable while it is stretching or feeling dry and tight.

Can I use it during pregnancy or while breastfeeding?
It is a 100% natural body butter with no synthetic colours or fragrances. As with any product, if you are pregnant or breastfeeding and at all unsure, check with your GP or midwife first.

Is it greasy or heavy on the skin?
It is a rich butter, but it absorbs quickly and does not leave a film behind. Use a small amount and massage it in fully, then add a little more to very dry patches.

What does it smell like?
Warm and citrusy, from natural orange and lemon oils rather than synthetic perfume, so it is light and fades as the butter sinks in.
