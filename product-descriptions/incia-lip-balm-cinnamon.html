INCIA Lip Balm Cinnamon is a 100% natural lip balm that wraps your lips in rich, lasting nourishment and a warm, subtly spicy cinnamon scent. Built around shea butter, coconut oil, castor oil and rosehip oil, it softens and protects dry, chapped lips while the gentle warmth of natural cinnamon makes every application feel like a cosy treat. There is no paraffin, no synthetic fragrance, no parabens and no artificial colours, just pure, natural lip care.

The blend of natural oils and butters delivers genuine, long-lasting moisture rather than a film that fades in minutes, so lips stay soft and comfortable through the day. That makes it a lovely pick for tough UK winters, when cold winds, dry indoor heating and chilly commutes leave lips cracked and sore. Rosehip oil brings natural vitamin C and vitamin E adds antioxidant support, while the warming cinnamon note lifts the whole experience.

How to use

Start with clean, dry lips so the balm settles evenly. Twist the base of the stick to bring the balm up, then glide a thin layer over your lips, working from the centre out to the corners. If you like, massage it in gently with small circular movements to help it absorb. Reapply through the day whenever lips feel dry or need extra care, especially in cold or windy weather.

Ingredients

A 100% natural blend of plant oils and butters. The full ingredients list is printed on the pack. Key natural ingredients include shea butter (Butyrospermum Parkii), coconut oil (Cocos Nucifera), castor oil (Ricinus Communis), rosehip oil (Rosa Canina), vitamin E (Tocopherol) and natural cinnamon for the warming scent.

  • Shea butter: rich in vitamins A and E, it intensively nourishes and softens lips.
  • Coconut oil: a deeply conditioning natural oil that helps keep lips supple.
  • Rosehip oil: a natural source of vitamin C, traditionally valued for conditioning delicate lip skin.
  • Castor oil: seals in moisture and gives the balm a natural glossy finish.

Frequently asked questions

Is it suitable for children as well as adults?
Yes. With 100% natural ingredients and no artificial additives, it suits both adults and children. With very young children we suggest supervising use. For a persistent concern, check with your GP or pharmacist.

What does the cinnamon scent smell like?
It is a warm, subtly spicy cinnamon note that comes from natural ingredients, not synthetic fragrance. If you know you are sensitive to spice notes or fragrance components, patch test first or check with your GP or pharmacist.

Can it be used on very dry or chapped lips?
Yes, it is well suited to dry and chapped lips. The natural butters and oils soften and comfort, helping to keep lips supple through cold, dry weather. If lips stay very cracked or sore, speak to your GP or pharmacist.

Why choose a natural balm over a paraffin-based one?
Many high-street balms rely on paraffin and synthetic additives that mainly sit on the surface. This natural blend of oils and butters is designed to give lasting comfort, so lips feel genuinely cared for rather than needing constant reapplication.
