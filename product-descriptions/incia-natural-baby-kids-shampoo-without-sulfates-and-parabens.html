INCIA Natural Baby & Kids Shampoo is a gentle, sulphate-free and paraben-free gel shampoo made for the delicate scalps of babies and children. Built on mild plant-based cleansers rather than harsh detergents, it cleans hair softly while working with the skin's natural balance, so young scalps feel comfortable rather than stripped. It is 100% natural, dermatologically tested and free from silicones, mineral oils, parabens and synthetic preservatives.

The gel formula makes dosing easy, so there is no waste and every wash uses just the right amount. Aloe vera and other soothing plant ingredients help care for dry, flaky scalps, and the same gentle blend doubles as a mild body wash for quick bath times. It is a practical everyday choice for sensitive skin, and hypoallergenic too, which makes it well suited to little ones who react to conventional shampoos.

How to use

Wet your child's hair, then apply a small amount of shampoo to the hair and body and massage in gently. Rinse thoroughly with plenty of warm water. Keep it away from the eyes where you can, and if any gets in, rinse with clean water. Gentle enough for daily use. For thicker or longer hair, follow with a conditioner if you like.

Frequently asked questions

Is it suitable for babies as well as older children?
Yes. The formula is gentle and free from synthetic additives, so it suits both babies and children. For a newborn, or if your baby has a specific skin concern, check with your GP, midwife or health visitor first.

Does it sting the eyes?
It is dermatologically tested and hypoallergenic, and designed to be mild. As with any shampoo, try to keep it out of the eyes, and rinse with clean water if any gets in.

Can I use it on sensitive or eczema-prone skin?
It is made for sensitive skin, with no sulphates, parabens or synthetic fragrance. If your child has eczema or a persistent skin concern, patch test first and check with your GP or pharmacist.

Can it be used as a body wash too?
Yes. The same gentle blend works as a mild body wash, so you can use one product for hair and body at bath time.
