INCIA Natural Hair Serum is a 100% natural treatment powered by ten botanical oils that work into the scalp and hair to support healthy-looking, glossy hair. This lightweight serum nourishes and hydrates both scalp and lengths, so hair looks fuller, stronger and more vibrant from root to tip, without any silicones, mineral oils or synthetic additives.

The oil blend is doing real work. Olive, avocado and sweet almond oils bring shine, while jojoba, rosemary and argan oils help condition and protect. Mastic gum and rosemary are traditional botanicals long associated with the scalp, and vitamin E adds antioxidant support. Unlike serums that rely on silicone to coat the hair for a temporary gloss, this natural blend is designed to soak in and nourish, leaving hair softer, smoother and less prone to frizz. It is vegan, dermatologically tested and comes in fully recyclable packaging.

How to use

Apply the serum to the scalp and hair once a week. Massage it in gently, then cover your hair and leave it to work for at least two hours, or overnight for a more intensive treatment. Rinse out thoroughly afterwards. Use it weekly for a month for the best results. For an even better effect, pair it with INCIA 100% Natural Shampoo. A little goes a long way with this concentrated 30ml serum.

Frequently asked questions

How often should I use it?
Once a week. Massage it into the scalp and hair, leave it on for at least two hours or overnight, then rinse thoroughly. Using it weekly for a month gives the best results.

Which hair types is it suitable for?
All hair types, whether fine, thick, curly or colour-treated. It is dermatologically tested and designed to be gentle enough for regular use, including on sensitive scalps.

Will it leave my hair greasy?
No. It is a lightweight, concentrated formula, so a little goes a long way. Applied as a treatment and rinsed out thoroughly, it conditions and adds shine without weighing hair down.

Is it natural and free from silicones?
Yes. It is 100% natural and vegan, with no silicones, mineral oils, parabens or synthetic fragrances, and it comes in fully recyclable packaging. If you have a scalp concern or notable hair thinning, check with your GP or pharmacist.
