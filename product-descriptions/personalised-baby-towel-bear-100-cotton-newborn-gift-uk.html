Care instructions

A gentle wash is recommended. Wash on a cool, gentle cycle, ideally with a mild, non-biological detergent, and avoid harsh bleaches so the cotton stays soft and the personalised label stays neat. Reshape while damp and dry naturally rather than on high heat. As with any soft keepsake, it is not intended to be left in a cot with a sleeping baby.
