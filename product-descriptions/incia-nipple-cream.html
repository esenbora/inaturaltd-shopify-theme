Dry, sensitive or sore nipples are a very common part of pregnancy and breastfeeding. The skin here is delicate, and it can quickly dry out or become chapped during feeding. The INCIA Nipple Cream is a completely water-free balm built on olive oil, beeswax and calendula oil, made to keep this delicate skin soft and comfortable while helping to guard against moisture loss.

It glides on smoothly and forms a gentle protective layer that helps lock in moisture. The balm is lanolin-free, free from synthetic additives, COSMOS Natural certified, hypoallergenic and dermatologically tested, and it is safe for both mother and baby during breastfeeding, so there is no need to wipe it off before every feed.

Key benefits

  • Protects the delicate nipple skin during pregnancy and breastfeeding
  • Helps guard against moisture loss and keeps skin soft
  • Completely lanolin-free, unlike many nipple balms
  • Safe for mother and baby during breastfeeding, with no need to wipe off before feeds
  • 100% natural, COSMOS Natural certified, hypoallergenic and dermatologically tested
  • Nourishing olive oil, beeswax, shea butter and calendula, with no synthetic additives

How to use

Apply a small amount to clean, dry nipples and the surrounding skin. Use after every feed, or more often as needed. If you prefer, any excess can be gently wiped away before feeding, though there is no need to. For persistent soreness or any concern while breastfeeding, check with your GP, pharmacist, midwife or health visitor.

Ingredients

Full ingredients: Olea Europaea Fruit Oil, Cera Alba, Soybean Glycerides, Butyrospermum Parkii Butter Unsaponifiables, Calendula Officinalis Flower Oil, Tocopherol.

  • Olive oil (Olea Europaea Fruit Oil): softens and nourishes delicate skin, helping it stay supple.

  • Shea butter (Butyrospermum Parkii Butter Unsaponifiables): nourishing and comforting, helping to care for chapped skin.
  • Calendula oil (Calendula Officinalis Flower Oil): long valued for its soothing, comforting feel on irritated skin.

Frequently asked questions

Is it safe to use during breastfeeding?
Yes. The balm is made with 100% natural ingredients, is lanolin-free and is safe for both mother and baby during breastfeeding, so there is no need to wipe it off before feeds. For any ongoing concern, check with your GP, midwife or health visitor.

Do I need to wipe it off before feeding?
No. It is safe to leave on for feeds. If you prefer, you can gently wipe away any excess beforehand, but it is not necessary.

Does it contain lanolin?
No. This balm is completely lanolin-free, which many parents prefer, and it is free from synthetic additives.

Can I use it on other dry, chapped areas?
Yes. Its nourishing, protective formula also suits other patches of dry or chapped skin, such as lips, cuticles and rough spots. For a persistent skin concern, see your GP or pharmacist.
