The INCIA Foaming Facial Cleanser is a mild, 100% natural face wash that lifts away dirt and make-up with just one pump of soft foam. Unlike many cleansers, it does not dry out the skin: thanks to added natural oils, it hydrates as it cleanses, so your face feels clean, soft and comfortable rather than tight. It is free from SLS and SLES and from synthetic colours and fragrances.

Its Super-Fat technology is the clever part: instead of stripping away your skin's natural oils, it is designed to keep as much of that protective layer intact as possible, helping to look after your skin barrier. The blend of olive and coconut oils dissolves impurities, squalane (which occurs naturally in skin) brings deep nourishment, and tea tree oil adds a fresh, clarifying touch. The result is a healthy-looking glow.

Key benefits

  • 100% natural face wash, with no SLS or SLES
  • Works as both a cleanser and a make-up remover
  • Hydrates while it cleanses, so skin never feels tight

  • Free from synthetic colours and fragrances, cruelty-free and vegan

How to use

Apply one pump of foam to a wet face. Massage in with gentle, circular motions for at least 20 seconds, then rinse well. Ideal for use in the shower, and it doubles as a make-up remover. Use it up to twice a day, morning and evening.

Ingredients

Full ingredients: Aqua, Olea Europaea (Olive) Fruit Oil, Potassium Hydroxide, Cocos Nucifera (Coconut) Oil, Acetic Acid, Ricinus Communis (Castor) Seed Oil, Sodium Hydroxide, Glycerin, Squalane, Melaleuca Alternifolia (Tea Tree) Leaf Oil.

  • Squalane: naturally present in skin, it deeply nourishes and helps keep skin soft and supple.
  • Olive and coconut oils: gently dissolve dirt and make-up while helping skin retain moisture.
  • Tea tree oil: adds a fresh, clarifying quality, which makes the cleanser a good fit for combination or blemish-prone skin.

Frequently asked questions

Is this cleanser suitable for all skin types?
It is developed especially for dry and sensitive skin. The tea tree oil also makes it a good choice for combination or blemish-prone skin. If you have a specific skin concern, check with your GP or pharmacist.

Can I use it twice a day, morning and evening?
Yes. The cleanser is gentle enough to use twice a day.

Does it remove make-up completely?
Yes. It doubles as a make-up remover and lifts away make-up as it cleanses.

Will the foam dry out my skin?
No. Thanks to its natural oils and Super-Fat technology, it cleanses without drying out the skin, leaving it soft and comfortable.
