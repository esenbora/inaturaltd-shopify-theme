The INCIA SOS Stick is a compact rescue balm for dry, rough or stressed skin, the one you keep in your bag for whenever skin needs a little help. It is made with eight cold-pressed plant oils, including sea buckthorn oil and shea butter, brought together into a solid balm that is 100% naturally made, with no synthetic ingredients, no fragrance and no fuss.

Sea buckthorn and St John's Wort quickly comfort skin, while shea butter and clary sage help lock in moisture. It supports the skin's natural barrier, so dry, flaky patches feel calmer and better looked after. Reach for it on rough elbows and knees, chapped cheeks, insect bites, minor scrapes and after time in the sun.

Key benefits

  • Eight cold-pressed plant oils in one compact stick
  • Comforts and soothes dry, irritated or sun-exposed skin
  • Supports the skin's natural moisture barrier

How to use

Glide the SOS Stick over the area that needs it and, if you like, massage the balm in. Use it as often as you want, on face and body. For a baby or very sensitive skin, patch test first.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use it every day on sensitive areas?
Yes. Use it as often as you like, on face and body. The formula is 100% natural, fragrance-free and free from synthetic ingredients, so it suits sensitive skin. For a persistent concern, check with your GP or pharmacist.

Are sea buckthorn and shea butter ok for very sensitive skin?
Both are mild and nourishing. No synthetic ingredients, fragrance or preservatives, so it works even for sensitive skin. For a serious skin condition, speak to your GP.

Does it help with redness or small dry patches?
It supports the skin's natural barrier, so dry or rough patches feel calmer. Handy for insect bites, scrapes and sun-exposed skin too. For ongoing concerns, see your GP or a dermatologist.

Is it fragrance-free?
Completely. No synthetic fragrance, nothing added; every ingredient is 100% naturally derived.
