How to use

Apply generously to clean, dry skin about 15 minutes before you go out in the sun, so it can settle. Glide the stick over face, ears, nose, lips, neck and the backs of the hands, then blend with your fingertips and check for missed patches. Reapply at least every 2 hours, and always straight after swimming, towelling or heavy sweating. Use shade, a hat and covered shoulders too: no sunscreen blocks 100% of UV.

Frequently asked questions

Is it suitable for babies and very sensitive skin?
It is 100% natural, dermatologically tested and uses mineral filters only, so it suits all skin types including the most sensitive. Babies under six months are best kept out of direct sunlight altogether rather than relying on sunscreen.

Why mineral filters rather than chemical ones?
Mineral filters stay on the surface as a protective layer and reflect UV light, which makes them a gentle option for delicate areas such as lips, eyes and nose, and for reapplying on children.

How often do I need to reapply?
At least every 2 hours in the sun, and immediately after swimming, towelling or sweating heavily. The stick makes that quick, which is the point of the format.

Can I really use it on my lips?
Yes. It is safe for lips and face and was developed for exactly those spots, so take it over the lip line, the nose and the tops of the ears.
