INCIA Natural Whitening Roll On Deodorant for Dark Armpits is a 100% natural deodorant made to look after the appearance of darker underarm skin while keeping you fresh all day. Darker underarms are very common, affecting roughly one in three people, and can be linked to shaving and hair removal, friction, tight clothing, hormonal changes such as pregnancy and a build-up of dead skin cells.

This roll-on pairs the natural mineral salt potassium alum, which has an antibacterial action against the bacteria behind body odour, with liquorice root extract, a plant extract known for its softening feel and for helping the underarm look more even in tone. It is kind and caring on the delicate skin under your arms, does not clog pores and leaves no marks on clothing.

How to use

Apply to clean, dry underarms. For the best result, roll it on the evening before so it can work overnight. Let it dry fully before getting dressed to help avoid transfer onto fabric. Consistent daily use gives the most even result over time.

Frequently asked questions

Does it really help with the look of dark underarms?
The formula contains liquorice root extract, a plant extract valued for its softening properties and for helping the underarm skin look more even in tone with regular use. Results vary from person to person. For a specific skin concern, check with your GP or pharmacist.

How long before I notice a difference in tone?
Give it consistency. Many people begin to see a visible effect after at least 28 days of regular daily use, so it is worth sticking with it.

Can I use it during pregnancy or breastfeeding?
The deodorant is made with 100% natural ingredients, with no synthetic aluminium, alcohol or preservatives. If you are pregnant or breastfeeding, we always recommend checking with your GP or midwife first.

Does it block the pores?
No. This is a deodorant, not an antiperspirant. It does not clog the pores or stop you sweating; it simply helps neutralise the bacteria that cause unpleasant odour.
