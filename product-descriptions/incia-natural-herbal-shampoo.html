INCIA Natural Herbal Shampoo is a 100% natural, vegan shampoo built around eight plant and herbal extracts that feed the scalp and hair from root to tip. Gathered using a gentle green extraction method, these botanicals cleanse thoroughly while helping to support healthy-looking hair growth, leaving hair feeling stronger and looking shinier and fuller.

Many conventional shampoos lean on silicones and synthetic conditioners that coat the hair for a quick shine while blocking out real nourishment. INCIA works differently, using plant-based cleansers such as coco-glucoside and lauryl glucoside alongside real herbal extracts, so hair is cared for without silicone build-up. It is dermatologically tested, hypoallergenic, free from silicones, parabens, SLS and SLES, artificial fragrances and mineral oils, and it comes in a 100% recyclable bottle.

How to use

Apply a small amount to wet hair, massage gently into the scalp and rinse thoroughly with water. For the best result, follow with INCIA Natural Hair Serum. Please note that hair can feel slightly stiff after use, so this shampoo is not recommended for curly hair. If you have curly or thicker hair, our Shrub Shampoo is a better match.

Frequently asked questions

Can it help with hair fall or thinning-looking hair?
The shampoo is developed with this in mind. Its eight herbal extracts, gathered by a green extraction method, include hops extract, known for supporting the hair roots, and horsetail extract, traditionally used to support healthy growth, while nettle nourishes and green tea adds shine and volume. For the best result, combine it with INCIA Natural Hair Serum. For ongoing hair or scalp concerns, check with your GP or pharmacist.

Is it suitable for children and during pregnancy?
The shampoo is dermatologically tested, hypoallergenic and made with 100% natural ingredients. For children we recommend the INCIA Kids Shampoo or Foaming Baby Shampoo, developed for delicate young skin. If you are pregnant and have any doubts, we always suggest checking with your GP or midwife first.

Does it contain sulphates, parabens or silicones?
No. The formula is free from SLS and SLES, parabens and silicones, using plant-based cleansers and real herbal extracts instead.

Is it suitable for all hair types, including curly or thick hair?
Hair can feel a little stiff after use, so this shampoo is not recommended for curly hair. For curly or thicker hair we suggest the Shrub Shampoo, developed for more intensive care and ideal alongside the Shrub Conditioner.
