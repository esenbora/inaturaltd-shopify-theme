INCIA Kids Lip Balm Orange is a 100% natural lip balm made for little lips, with a cheerful orange scent that turns lip care into a treat children actually want. Made with simple, plant-based ingredients such as olive oil, beeswax, shea butter, rosehip oil and coconut oil, it keeps young lips soft and comforted with no synthetic fragrances, no paraffin and no artificial colours.

Children's lips chap easily because they have no oil-producing glands of their own, so they lose moisture quickly in cold, dry or sunny weather. This gentle stick lays down a light, protective layer that helps lock in moisture, so your child's lips stay soft and cared for through the school run, the playground and family holidays. Because every ingredient is naturally derived, a little accidental lick is nothing to worry about.

Key benefits

  • 100% natural formula, made with children in mind
  • Beeswax forms a gentle protective barrier against wind and cold
  • Shea butter, coconut and rosehip oils nourish and soften delicate lips
  • Fun, naturally derived orange scent, no synthetic fragrance or colour
  • Free from paraffin, parabens and artificial additives
  • Pocket-sized 6g stick, perfect for school bags and sports kits

How to use

Make sure your child's lips are clean and dry, then twist the base of the stick to bring the balm up. Help them glide a thin, even layer over the lips, starting in the middle and working out to the corners. Reapply through the day whenever lips feel dry, especially in cold, windy or sunny weather. With very young children, apply it for them or supervise closely.

Ingredients

Full ingredients: Olea Europaea Fruit Oil, Cera Alba, Ricinus Communis (Castor) Seed Oil, Butyrospermum Parkii (Shea) Butter, Cocos Nucifera (Coconut) Oil, Rosa Canina Fruit Oil, Tocopherol, Citrus Sinensis (Orange) Peel Oil.

  • Beeswax (Cera Alba): forms a light protective layer that helps keep lips hydrated and shielded from wind and cold.
  • Shea butter: nourishing and softening, it helps protect against everyday environmental factors.
  • Coconut oil: a deeply conditioning natural oil that helps keep young lips supple.
  • Orange peel oil: gives the balm its bright, naturally derived citrus scent.

Frequently asked questions

From what age can my child use it?
It is made with 100% natural ingredients and no artificial additives, so it suits children. With very young children we suggest you apply it for them or supervise use. If your child has a persistent lip concern, check with your GP or pharmacist.

Is it free from synthetic fragrances and colours?
Yes. There are no artificial fragrances or colours. The scent comes entirely from the natural orange oil in the formula.

Can it be used on very dry or chapped lips?
Yes. The beeswax forms a protective layer that helps prevent moisture loss, while shea butter, coconut oil and rosehip oil help soften and comfort dry lips. For lips that stay very sore or cracked, speak to your GP or pharmacist.

Is it safe if my child accidentally licks it off?
Every ingredient is naturally derived, so a small amount licked or swallowed is nothing to worry about. It is still meant for the lips, not for eating, so supervise younger children. If in doubt, check with your GP.
