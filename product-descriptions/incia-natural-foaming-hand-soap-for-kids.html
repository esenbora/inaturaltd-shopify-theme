How to use

Press the pump once for the perfect amount of foaming soap, then wash hands as usual and rinse. The ready-made foam means there is no need to work up a lather, which is ideal for small hands.

Frequently asked questions

Is the soap mild enough for children with sensitive skin?
Yes. It is free from parabens, sulphates and synthetic additives, and it keeps the natural glycerin released during the soap-making process, which helps care for the skin while washing. For a specific skin concern, check with your GP or pharmacist.

Why does it sometimes foam a little less?
The foam comes from natural ingredients rather than synthetic foaming agents, so it is naturally a touch softer and less exuberant than the lather from synthetic soaps. That is completely normal and does not affect how well it cleans.

Is it safe if my child swallows a small amount by accident?
The soap is made with 100% natural ingredients, but it is not intended to be swallowed. If your child swallows more than a tiny amount or you have any concerns, contact your GP or pharmacist.

What if the pump sticks or stops working?
Give it a few firm presses to prime it. If it still will not dispense, please get in touch and we will be happy to help.
