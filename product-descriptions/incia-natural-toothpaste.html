How to use

Apply a pea-sized amount of toothpaste to a toothbrush and brush the teeth gently in small circular movements. For the smallest babies, an amount the size of a grain of rice is plenty. As with any toothpaste, supervise young children while they brush. For any concern about your child's teeth or gums, speak to your dentist.

Frequently asked questions

From what age can babies and children use this toothpaste?
It is suitable from the moment the first milk teeth come through, and it works for the whole family too. As always, supervise young children while they brush.

Is it safe if a baby or child swallows a little?
The formula contains no synthetic preservatives, using cinnamon bark extract as a natural preservative instead, and it is free from SLS and SLES. Use a small amount, a grain of rice for babies or a pea-sized amount for children, and supervise brushing. For specific advice, speak to your dentist.

How much should I use each time?
Apply a pea-sized amount to the toothbrush for children, or an amount the size of a grain of rice for the smallest babies.

Is it really fluoride-free?
Yes. This is a fluoride-free formula. If you would like tailored advice on fluoride for your family, your dentist is the best person to ask.
