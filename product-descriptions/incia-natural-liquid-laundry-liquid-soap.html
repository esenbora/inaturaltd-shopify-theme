How to use

Sort your laundry by colour and fabric type. For a normal load, measure 30ml using the cap and pour it into the detergent drawer, then load the machine as the manufacturer advises, choose your programme and start. For stubborn marks, dab a little directly onto the spot before washing. No extra rinse is needed. Store in a cool, dry place and shake gently before use.

Ingredients

Full ingredients: Aqua, Sodium Cocoate, Sodium Carbonate, Sodium Citrate, Lavandula Angustifolia Oil, Glycerin.

  • Coconut soap (Sodium Cocoate): a plant-based soap with strong grease- and grime-lifting power, one of the most effective cleansers nature offers.
  • Sodium carbonate: a natural mineral that supports cleaning by helping to neutralise acids and lift residues.
  • Lavender oil: gives laundry a soft, fresh, naturally calming scent without synthetic fragrance.
  • Glycerin: a gentle, plant-derived humectant that keeps the formula mild.

Frequently asked questions

My liquid has turned slightly yellow or cloudy. Can I still use it?
Yes, this is completely normal. Because there are no artificial colours or preservatives, a little discolouration or sediment can appear over time. It is a natural process and does not affect how the product works. Simply shake well before use.

Why does my bottle smell a little different from last time?
Because we work only with natural ingredients, the scent can vary slightly from batch to batch. This is normal with natural products and says nothing about the quality or performance of the liquid.

Why does it say "baby" on the pack? Is it also for adults?
Yes, it is suitable for everyone. The word "baby" simply highlights how mild and gentle the formula is, which also makes it ideal for adults with sensitive skin.

My washing smells musty afterwards. What can I do?
A musty smell often comes from leftover moisture or a dirty machine rather than the detergent. Clean your washing machine about once a month with natural vinegar or soda, avoid overdosing the liquid, and leave the door open after each wash so the drum can dry.
