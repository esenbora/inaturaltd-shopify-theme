The INCIA Natural Sunscreen Lotion for All Family is a mineral SPF 50+ sunscreen the whole household can share, from babies to grown-ups. It uses non-nano, Ecocert-certified titanium dioxide as its mineral UV filter, which sits on the surface of the skin and reflects UVA and UVB rays rather than being absorbed the way chemical filters are.

The lightweight lotion is 100% natural, hypoallergenic and dermatologically tested, and it is nourished with cold-pressed plant oils that help care for the skin as you wear it. Free from synthetic fragrance, parabens and mineral oils, it suits every skin type from sensitive and eczema-prone to oily, and it is waterproof for time in and out of the water.

How to use

Pump a small amount of sunscreen into your palm and smooth a thin, even layer over the whole body, using roughly a teaspoon per body part. Spread it well. Reapply every two to three hours for continued protection, and always after swimming, towelling off or heavy perspiration. As with any mineral sunscreen, take care to avoid contact with fabrics.

Frequently asked questions

Is the sunscreen suitable for babies, children and adults?
Yes. It is made for the whole family, from babies to adults. The formula is hypoallergenic and free from synthetic additives. For very young or very sensitive skin, we always suggest a patch test first.

Does it leave a white cast?
Because it is a mineral sunscreen with titanium dioxide as the filter, you may notice a light white sheen after applying. This is completely normal with mineral sunscreens. Applying a thin layer and spreading it well helps keep it to a minimum.

Is it water-resistant?
Yes, it is waterproof. For best results, reapply every two to three hours, and after swimming, towelling off or heavy perspiration.

Is it suitable for sensitive skin?
Yes. It is hypoallergenic, dermatologically tested and free from synthetic fragrance. If your skin is very sensitive, patch test on a small area first, and speak to your GP or pharmacist about any ongoing concern.
