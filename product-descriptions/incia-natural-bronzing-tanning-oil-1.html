How to use

Sunscreen comes first, always. If you are out in the sun, apply your broad-spectrum SPF as directed and reapply at least every 2 hours and after swimming. Many people keep this oil for shaded time, evenings and after a shower, when its only job is to nourish skin and bring a natural sheen. Warm a small amount between your palms and smooth a thin layer over the body. The oil is not water resistant, so reapply after swimming or sweating. If skin feels hot or tight, or looks pink, get out of the sun and cover up.

Frequently asked questions

Does it protect me from the sun?
No. It contains no SPF and gives no sun protection at all. You need a separate broad-spectrum sunscreen for that, applied generously and reapplied at least every 2 hours. Have a look at our natural sun care range, including the SPF 50+ mineral sunscreen stick.

Will it make me tan faster?
It is not designed to rush anything. Beta-carotene rich oils give skin a warm golden look, but a tan is your skin reacting to UV exposure and there is no safe way to speed that up. If your skin is fair, keep exposure short and wear sunscreen daily.

Is it water resistant?
No. Reapply after swimming, towel-drying or heavy sweating, and reapply your sunscreen at the same time.

Is it suitable for sensitive skin?
It suits all skin types, including sensitive skin, and is free from synthetic additives. Patch test first if your skin is easily irritated.
