The INCIA Natural Remedy for Insect Bites is a 100% natural repellent lotion that helps keep mosquitoes, ticks, midges and other biting insects at bay, without a drop of DEET. With UK summers bringing more midges, mosquitoes and the risk of tick-borne illness, a natural way to protect the whole family outdoors has become a genuine essential.

The lotion is built around micro-encapsulated lemon eucalyptus oil, a natural insect-repelling oil that is released slowly and steadily so the effect lasts through the day. Squalane and vitamin E add hydrating, skin-softening qualities, so unlike many repellents it looks after your skin rather than drying it out. Suitable for babies, children and adults.

  • Micro-encapsulated lemon eucalyptus oil for a controlled, longer-lasting release
  • Helps keep skin protected for up to six hours

How to use

Apply the lotion to exposed skin. Use a small amount each time and spread it well across the area. Reapply every six hours for continued protection, and more often if you have been swimming, towelling off or perspiring heavily. This is a repellent for intact skin rather than a treatment for existing bites, so avoid open or broken skin. For a bite that has already happened, or for grazes and chapped skin, reach for the INCIA SOS Stick instead.

Frequently asked questions

Is it safe to use during pregnancy or breastfeeding?
The product is made with 100% natural ingredients and contains no DEET. It does, however, contain essential oils such as lemon eucalyptus oil and cinnamon extract. If you are pregnant or breastfeeding, please check with your GP, pharmacist or midwife before use.

Does it contain fragrance or anything likely to irritate?
There is no synthetic fragrance and no parabens. The scent comes purely from the natural ingredients in the formula, including lemon eucalyptus oil. If your skin is sensitive, we always suggest patch testing on a small area first.

How quickly does it work, and how long does it last?
This is primarily a preventive repellent to keep insects away rather than something to ease itching after a bite. The protective effect lasts roughly two to six hours on average, depending on how much you use and the conditions around you.

Can I use it on broken skin or scratched bites?
We would not recommend it. The lotion is designed as a protective product for intact skin. For open or broken skin, and for scrapes and insect bites, the INCIA SOS Stick is the better choice.
