The INCIA 100% Natural Easy Combing Spray is made to turn after-bath detangling into a calm, tear-free moment for you and your little one. How to use

Shake well before use. Spray evenly over wet or dry hair, whichever you prefer, then gently comb through. There is no need to rinse: it is made to stay in the hair for a lasting effect. For a little extra care, follow with a natural baby oil as a finishing touch.

Ingredients

Full ingredients: Aqua, Butylene Glycol, Glycerin, PCA Ethyl Cocoyl Arginate, Triethyl Citrate, Cinnamomum Zeylanicum Bark Extract, Caprylyl Glycol, Parfum, Linalool, Limonene.

  • PCA Ethyl Cocoyl Arginate: a gentle coconut-derived conditioning active that softens hair and helps loosen tangles without weighing it down.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use it on both wet and dry hair?
Yes. Spray it onto wet or dry hair and comb through gently. No rinsing needed: the spray is designed to stay in the hair for a lasting, smoothing effect.

Does it work on curly or thick hair?
Yes. It suits all hair types, including fine, wavy and curly hair. The coconut amino acid complex bonds to the hair and eases out tangles without making it feel heavy.

Is it safe for children and sensitive scalps?
Yes. The ingredients are pure and carefully chosen with children and the environment in mind. It is free from alcohol, silicones and synthetic actives, and made with young scalps in mind. If your child has a scalp condition, check with your GP or pharmacist first.

Is it really 100% natural?
Yes. Every ingredient is naturally derived, with no petrochemicals, silicones or artificial colours, and the packaging is fully recyclable.
