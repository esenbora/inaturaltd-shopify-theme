Frequently asked questions

What exactly comes in the box?
Four full products: the Eyelash Serum for eyelashes and eyebrows, the Coconut Butter, the Nails and Cuticles Care Balm and the Natural Deodorant for Sensitive Skin, presented together in festive gift packaging.

Does it need wrapping?
No. It is designed as a gift set and comes in its own festive packaging, so it is ready to give straight away.

Is it suitable for sensitive skin?
Each product is naturally formulated and free from artificial fragrances and colours, and the deodorant was developed specifically for sensitive, easily irritated underarms. If the recipient has very reactive skin, suggest a patch test first.

Can the deodorant be used during pregnancy?
It is suitable for use during pregnancy and breastfeeding. As with any product, if you or the recipient are pregnant and at all unsure, it is worth checking with a GP or midwife.

Are the products cruelty-free?
Yes. INCIA does not test on animals, and that applies to every product in this set.
