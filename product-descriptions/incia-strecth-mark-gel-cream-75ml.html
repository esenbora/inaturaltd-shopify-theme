Many of us have stretch marks. They appear as fine streaks on the skin when the connective tissue beneath is stretched, which is why they are so common during pregnancy. The INCIA Stretch Mark Gel Cream was developed by INCIA especially for use through pregnancy, to help keep skin nourished, supple and cared for as it changes.

This rich, 100% natural cream deeply hydrates and helps reduce moisture loss, so skin stays soft and comfortable. It is designed to help prevent the appearance of stretch marks and to support the skin's suppleness, and it can be used throughout pregnancy and for up to three months afterwards to support recovery. Whether you are pregnant, going through weight changes or simply looking after dry skin, it delivers intensive nourishment.

Key benefits

  • Helps prevent the appearance of stretch marks (striae)
  • Deeply hydrates and helps reduce moisture loss
  • Supports the skin's suppleness and elasticity as it stretches
  • Rich in shea butter, sweet almond and avocado oils for a natural, nourished feel
  • Safe to use during pregnancy and for up to three months afterwards
  • Made with 100% natural ingredients, with no synthetic fragrances or colourants

How to use

Massage the cream into the skin throughout pregnancy to help head off the development of stretch marks, focusing on the tummy, hips, thighs, bottom and bust. Daily use through pregnancy gives the best preventive results, and the cream may also be used for up to three months after birth to support the skin as it recovers. If you have any questions about use during pregnancy, check with your GP, pharmacist or midwife.

Frequently asked questions

Is the cream safe during pregnancy and breastfeeding?
Yes. The cream was developed especially for use during pregnancy and is suitable from the third month onwards, and for up to three months after birth. If you are ever unsure, check with your GP, pharmacist or midwife.

Does it reduce existing stretch marks, or is it mainly preventive?
Both. Its main role is preventive, keeping skin hydrated and supple, but it also helps support the look of existing stretch marks thanks to its naturally derived actives. Results with existing marks vary from person to person.

Is it suitable for sensitive skin or eczema?
It is made with 100% natural ingredients and no synthetic additives, though it does contain soy-derived ingredients. If your skin is sensitive or prone to eczema, patch test on a small area first, and speak to a GP or dermatologist if you have concerns.

How long until I see results with regular use?
For the best preventive results, apply daily throughout pregnancy. With existing stretch marks, the visible improvement varies from person to person, depending on skin structure and how established the marks are.
