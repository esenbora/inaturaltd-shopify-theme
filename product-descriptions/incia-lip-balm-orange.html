INCIA Lip Balm Orange is a 100% natural moisturising lip balm that keeps your lips soft, nourished and protected all day, finished with a bright, uplifting orange scent. It is built entirely from plant-based oils and butters, with shea butter, coconut oil, castor oil and rosehip oil doing the work and vitamin E along for the ride. No paraffin, no synthetic fragrance, no artificial colour.

Beeswax is the quiet hero here: it lays down a fine, breathable layer that helps stop lips drying out and shields them from harsh weather, whether that is winter wind, dry central heating or strong summer sun. The rich blend of natural oils keeps lips glossy and comfortable, so instead of a quick film that fades in minutes, you get genuine, lasting nourishment. Naturally derived and kind enough for sensitive skin.

How to use

Start with clean, dry lips so the balm settles evenly. Twist the base of the stick to bring the balm up, then glide a thin layer over your lips, working from the centre out to the corners. If you like, massage it in gently with small circular movements to help it absorb. Reapply through the day whenever lips feel dry or need extra care, especially in cold or windy weather.

Ingredients

Full ingredients: Butyrospermum Parkii (Shea) Butter, Cocos Nucifera (Coconut) Oil, Ricinus Communis (Castor) Seed Oil, Cera Alba, Rosa Canina Fruit Oil, Tocopherol, Citrus Sinensis (Orange) Peel Oil.

  • Shea butter: rich in vitamins A and E, it nourishes and softens while helping shield lips from environmental factors.
  • Beeswax (Cera Alba): forms a protective layer that helps keep lips hydrated in warm, cold or sunny conditions.
  • Rosehip oil: a natural source of vitamin C that helps support the look and feel of lip skin.
  • Orange peel oil: gives the balm its fresh, naturally derived citrus scent.

Frequently asked questions

Is it suitable for children as well as adults?
Yes. It is 100% natural with no artificial additives, so it suits both adults and children. With very young children we suggest supervising use. For a persistent concern, check with your GP or pharmacist.

Is it free from synthetic fragrances and colours?
Completely. There are no artificial fragrances or colours; the scent comes entirely from the natural orange oil in the formula.

Can it be used on very dry or chapped lips?
Yes. The beeswax forms a protective barrier that helps prevent moisture loss, while shea butter, coconut oil and rosehip oil intensively soften and comfort. If lips stay very cracked or sore, speak to your GP or pharmacist.

Why choose a natural balm over a paraffin-based one?
Many high-street balms rely on paraffin and synthetic additives. This 100% natural blend of oils and butters is designed to give lasting comfort rather than just a temporary film, so lips feel genuinely cared for.
