Frequently asked questions

Why does the pH of 3.8 matter?
Intimate skin naturally sits at a slightly acidic pH. A wash balanced to 3.8 cleanses gently while helping to support your skin's natural defences, rather than disturbing them.

Can I use it every day?
Yes. It is designed for gentle daily use, and is especially refreshing during your period, or after sport and swimming.

Is it suitable for sensitive skin?
The formula is 100% natural, with soothing aloe vera and lavender and no harsh synthetic ingredients, so it is a gentle choice for sensitive skin. If you have a known sensitivity or an ongoing concern, check with your GP or pharmacist first.

Is it only for internal or external use?
It is for the external intimate area only. Apply a small amount and rinse off under running water.
