INCIA Nail and Cuticle Care Balm is a small tin of care for the bit of your routine that usually gets forgotten. Made from 11 different natural oils, it is a simple, completely natural balm for dry, cracked cuticles and for keeping nails looking strong and glossy, on hands and feet alike.

The cuticle, that thin strip of skin where nail meets finger, is delicate, and it has a job to do: it acts as a barrier for the nail underneath. When it dries out it can crack and split, which is where those painful hangnails come from, and dry cuticles do nail growth no favours either. The natural oils in this balm help cuticles and nails hold onto moisture by forming a soft protective layer over them, so the skin around your nails stays supple and comfortable. 15ml tin.

How to use

Apply the balm to your nails and cuticles a few times a week, or daily if your hands take a lot of punishment. A thin layer is all you need: take a small amount on a fingertip, smooth it around the nail and the cuticle line, then massage it in for a few seconds so it settles. It is suitable for fingernails and toenails, and it spreads very easily. Evening is a good time, since the oils have all night to work.

Ingredients

The balm is made from 11 types of natural oil and nothing else of note: no synthetic fragrance, no artificial colour. Those natural oils are what moisturise the cuticles and nails, creating a light protective layer over the nail plate and the surrounding skin that helps retain moisture and keeps the skin supple and nourished. Frequently asked questions

Can I use it after gel, BIAB or shellac?
Yes. It is completely safe to use after your manicure and will not affect the polish. Nail technicians usually recommend keeping cuticles moisturised precisely because well-hydrated cuticles help the finish last longer.

How often should I use it?
A few times a week is enough for most people. If your hands are often in water, soap or cleaning products, use it daily, and keep the tin somewhere you will actually see it.

Will it help with hangnails?
The skin around the nail takes constant wear from water, soap and everyday use, and it splits when it dries out. Keeping it oiled and supple with this balm makes that dry, ragged skin far less likely. If a hangnail becomes sore, swollen or does not settle, see your GP or pharmacist.

Is it greasy on the hands?
Used as intended, no. A thin layer massaged in is enough for full nail care, so you are not left with slippery fingers.

Can I use it on my toenails too?
Yes. It is made for both, and toenails and the skin around them benefit just as much, especially in sandal season and after a pedicure.
