INCIA Kids Lip Balm Lemon is a natural lip balm made especially for children, with a bright, naturally derived lemon scent that little ones actually want to put on. Every ingredient is naturally derived, with no artificial fragrances and no artificial colours, so it is gentle on delicate young lips and simple enough for a child to twist up and apply themselves.

Children's lips have no oil-producing glands of their own, so they lose moisture quickly and chap easily in cold, dry or sunny weather. This soft balm lays down a comforting, protective layer that helps keep lips soft, glossy and well looked after through busy days at nursery, school and play. It is made with food-safe natural ingredients, so it is safe if a child licks their lips.

How to use

Make sure your child's lips are clean and dry, then twist the base of the stick to bring the balm up. Help them apply a thin, even layer, starting in the middle of the lips and working outwards with light pressure so it spreads evenly. Reapply through the day whenever lips feel dry, and especially before going outside in cold, windy or sunny weather. With very young children, apply it for them or supervise closely.

Frequently asked questions

From what age can my child use it?
It is made especially for children, with natural ingredients and no artificial additives. With very young children we suggest you apply it for them or supervise use. For a persistent lip concern, check with your GP or pharmacist.

Is it free from artificial fragrances and colours?
Yes. There are no artificial fragrances or colours; the scent comes entirely from natural ingredients.

Is it safe if my child licks it off?
It is made with food-safe natural ingredients, so it is safe if a child licks their lips or swallows a little. It is still meant for the lips rather than for eating, so supervise younger children, and if in doubt check with your GP.

Can it be used on very dry or chapped lips?
Yes. The balm gives dry, chapped lips a soft protective layer that helps them hold onto moisture, which is exactly what lips need in hot or cold weather and after time in the sun. If lips stay very sore, speak to your GP or pharmacist.

Do you do other flavours?
Yes. The same formula comes in a strawberry version, so you can let your child pick their favourite. Both sit neatly in our lip balm collection.
