The INCIA Eyelash Serum for Lashes and Brows is a 100% natural conditioning serum for fuller-looking lashes and beautifully groomed brows. Blending cold-pressed plant oils rich in fatty acids, vitamins and minerals, it nourishes lashes and brows and helps them look their healthiest. Because the oils are cold-pressed, their goodness is captured in optimal condition.

The star ingredient is castor oil, often called wonder oil, which is prized for its ability to hold onto moisture and condition and soften lash and brow hairs. Alongside it you will find sesame, sweet almond, argan and apricot kernel oils, all rich in antioxidants that help protect hairs against everyday environmental stress. The serum absorbs quickly and helps strengthen the look of lashes and brows against breakage, which also makes it a lovely base before shaping your brows.

How to use

Before bed, apply one drop of the serum with the pipette to your lashes and brows, then massage in gently with your fingertips. Many people notice a difference within one to three weeks of regular use. Please note: if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, take extra care on first use, as hormonal changes can sometimes make skin react unexpectedly, and check with your GP or midwife first.

Frequently asked questions

Is the serum suitable for sensitive eyes?
The serum is made purely from 100% natural plant oils and vitamin E, with no synthetic additives or fragrance. If your eyes are sensitive, apply carefully so the serum does not go directly into the eye. If in doubt, do a patch test first.

How often should I apply it to see a difference?
Apply the serum every evening before bed. With regular use, many people notice the first results within one to three weeks.

Is it safe to use during pregnancy or breastfeeding?
The product is 100% natural. Even so, we recommend taking care on first use, as hormonal changes during pregnancy can sometimes cause unexpected reactions. We always suggest discussing it with your GP or midwife first.

Can I use it on my brows as well as my lashes?
Yes. It doubles as a brow serum and makes a lovely conditioning base before shaping and grooming your brows.
