What is the INCIA SOS Stick?

The SOS Stick is a compact, twist-up balm that soothes and helps skin recover quickly. It's formulated with cold-pressed plant oils and botanical extracts — no parabens, no silicones, no synthetic fragrance — so it's gentle enough for the whole family, including babies and children.

Key ingredients do the heavy lifting:

  • Calendula — a classic botanical for calming irritated, reactive skin.
  • Chamomile — helps soothe visible redness.
  • Shea butter — deeply nourishes and softens dry, tight skin.
  • Beeswax — forms a light protective barrier that locks in moisture.

When to reach for it

Keep one within arm's reach and you'll find yourself using it constantly:

  • Insect bites and stings that need calming fast
  • Minor burns and grazes
  • Dry, cracked patches on hands, elbows and heels
  • Chapped lips and wind-burnt cheeks
  • Rough cuticles and hangnails

How to use the SOS Stick for best results

  1. Clean first. Gently cleanse the area with a mild soap and lukewarm water, then pat dry.
  2. Apply a thin layer. Twist up the stick and glide it directly over the skin — a little goes a long way.
  3. Repeat as needed. For persistent dryness or irritation, reapply 2–3 times a day.
  4. Keep it clear of eyes and open wounds. It's a first-response balm, not a treatment for deep cuts.
  5. Store somewhere cool and dry so the balm keeps its smooth, glide-on texture.

Tips to get more from your stick

Apply at the first sign of irritation rather than waiting — the sooner skin is soothed and protected, the quicker it settles. For very dry areas, use it last thing at night so it can work while you sleep. And because it's pocket-sized, stash one in every bag so you're never caught out.
