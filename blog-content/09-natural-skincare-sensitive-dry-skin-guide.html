What triggers dryness and irritation in the first place?

Dry, reactive skin is rarely down to one thing. Cold British winters and central heating pull moisture out of the skin. Hot showers, harsh soaps and over-washing strip away the natural oils that keep it comfortable. Synthetic fragrance, alcohol and sulphates are common culprits behind stinging and redness. And some people simply have skin that runs dry or reacts easily, including eczema-prone skin, which tends to feel itchy, rough and tight. Working out your own triggers helps, and if flare-ups are frequent or severe, that is one to talk through with your GP or pharmacist.

Why does the skin barrier matter so much?

Your skin has a natural outer layer, often called the moisture barrier, that holds water in and keeps irritants out. When it is working well, skin feels soft and calm. When it is compromised, by harsh products, weather or over-cleansing, moisture escapes and irritants get in more easily, so skin feels dry, tight and reactive. Almost everything below comes back to one idea: look after the barrier rather than fighting it. Gentle formulas that support the skin's natural barrier, instead of stripping it, are the foundation of any routine for sensitive skin.

Why do fewer, simpler ingredients help?

When skin reacts easily, every extra ingredient is another thing that might set it off. That is why a shorter, simpler routine usually beats a shelf full of products. A few labels worth looking for on sensitive skin:

  • Dermatologically tested and hypoallergenic. Screened to keep the risk of a reaction low.
  • No SLS, SLES, parabens or synthetic fragrance. These are the usual triggers behind stinging and redness.
  • Free from alcohol and paraffin. So a formula moisturises without drying skin out or smothering it.
  • Made with natural plant oils. Ingredients that nourish and comfort rather than just sit on the surface.
