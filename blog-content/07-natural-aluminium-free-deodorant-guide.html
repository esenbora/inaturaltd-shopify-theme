Deodorant or antiperspirant: what is the difference?

This is the bit that clears up most of the confusion. An antiperspirant tries to stop sweat. A deodorant does not; it lets your body perspire naturally and instead works on the odour. That distinction matters, because sweating is how your body cools and cleanses itself, and blocking it is exactly what a lot of people are looking to avoid. Both INCIA formulas are deodorants, not antiperspirants, so they will not block your pores or stop you sweating. They help control the smell, which for most of us is the actual goal.

How does a natural deodorant control odour?

Here is the part that surprises people: fresh sweat does not smell. Odour only appears when sweat meets the everyday bacteria living on your skin. Tackle those bacteria and you tackle the smell at its source. INCIA does this with potassium alum, a natural mineral salt used for generations, which has an antibacterial action against the bacteria behind body odour. No pore-blocking, no aluminium salts, just a plant-and-mineral approach to keeping you fresh. It is a simpler idea than it sounds, and it holds up well even on busier days.

What is the adjustment period, and how do I get through it?

When you come off an antiperspirant, your underarms carry on perspiring at their usual rate for a little while as they settle back into a natural rhythm. Most people find this evens out within a couple of weeks. A few things make it smoother:

  • Give it consistency. Daily use is where natural deodorant earns its keep.
  • Apply to clean, dry skin, ideally the evening before, so it can work overnight.
  • Let it dry fully before you dress, which also stops any transfer onto fabric.
  • Reapply on very active days rather than expecting a single application to do everything.
