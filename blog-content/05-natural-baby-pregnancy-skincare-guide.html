What actually makes a product safe for babies and pregnancy?

Newborn skin is thin and reactive, and it soaks up more than adult skin does. In pregnancy, hormones tend to leave skin drier and itchier than usual. The answer is the same either way: fewer ingredients, nothing harsh, and formulas that have been tested on sensitive skin. A few things worth looking for on the label:

  • Dermatologically tested and hypoallergenic. Screened to keep the risk of a reaction low.
  • No SLS, parabens or synthetic fragrance. These are usually the culprits behind irritation in young skin.
  • Ecocert-certified ingredients. An independent check that the label matches what's really in the bottle.
  • Vegan and cruelty-free. No animal ingredients, no animal testing.

Sun protection for babies and children

Under six months, keep babies out of direct sun altogether. For older babies and children, go for a mineral (physical) sunscreen. It sits on top of the skin and reflects UV rather than being absorbed, which makes it the kinder choice. The INCIA Natural Sunscreen for Baby and Child SPF50 gives broad-spectrum cover in a formula built for delicate skin. Reapply every couple of hours and after they've been in water, and don't lean on it alone. A hat and some shade in the middle of the day do a lot of the work.
